# 0061. Every live fragment stays on screen, the stop is acknowledged at once, the passes say they are queued, and a meeting is renamed from its title

Status: Accepted 2026-09-16, amends 0038 (the provisional tail) and 0035 and 0036 (the pass statuses)

## What this gives you

The live transcript shows every sentence the engine has for the last few seconds, not only the newest one. Pressing Stop turns the screen at once: the recording square goes muted, the elapsed display freezes on the recorded length, and a `Recording stopped` line with a progress bar counts the transcription's chunks until the meeting opens. The opened meeting says which passes are still to come, `Transcript ready · speakers queued`, with the transcript usable underneath, and it says when the analysis failed instead of looking finished. The title at the top of a meeting is the rename control: click it or press `t`, type, Return saves, Escape keeps the saved name.

## Situation

A 52-minute dogfood on 2026-09-16 (spec fn-63) showed three defects. The merger emits a window's provisional tail as several fragments (`you-p1`, `you-p2`, ...) and the daemon publishes each one (`crates/dettivo-meeting/src/live.rs`), while `LiveSegmentsModel` kept one provisional segment per source as ADR 0038 stated, so every fragment but the last vanished until the finalisation put it back; 74 of 74 overwritten fragments were found intact in the settled segments, so the loss was display only. Stop set `finishing` only when `meetings.stop` answered, the clock kept running, and the only progress sign was a caption in the meters row, so the stop read as ignored. After `completed` the detail opened with `diarization_status` and `analysis_status` still `none` for the seconds until each pass published `running`, and the speaker pass showed as a muted hint under the talk-time bar; an analysis that failed with `invalid_output` left the rail saying `No analysis yet`, indistinguishable from a meeting that never ran one. The contract had no way to rename a meeting after the start.

## Decision

- **The tail keeps every fragment and the wire says how.** `meeting.segment` now documents the true rule (`crates/dettivo-proto/src/events.rs`): `<source>-p1` opens a fresh provisional tail for its source and drops the earlier one, a later `-pN` replaces the fragment with its id or extends the tail, and a final segment retires the fragments of its source that start before its end. `LiveSegmentsModel::apply` implements exactly that; a provisional id without a fragment number stands for the whole tail, so a client that sends one segment per source keeps working.
- **Stop is acknowledged before the daemon answers.** `MeetingLiveModel::stop` sets `finishing`, stops the clock and zeroes the meters as it sends `meetings.stop`; a refusal releases the flag, restarts the clock and lands in `error`, which the header shows in the urgent colour. The header replaces the meters with `Recording stopped`, the stage line (`Stopping · closing the takes`, `Transcribing · 18 of 22 chunks`) and a `ProgressBar` that sweeps until `job.progress` carries a chunk total. The Stop button stays disabled while finishing, as before.
- **The finalisation writes the plan on the row before it publishes `completed`.** `MeetingArchive::completed` marks the diarization block `queued` when `Diarization::planned(config, row)` says the pass will run and the analysis `queued` when `Analysis::wants_auto` does, before the row is stored and the `completed` transition goes out, so `meetings.get` on that transition already carries both. `AnalysisStatus` and `DiarizationStatus` gain `queued` (wire spelling `queued`; migration `0008-analysis-queued` widens the column check). `auto_start` and `settled` run from the same answers; a plan that cannot launch is settled `failed` with the reason, and `fail_stale_meeting_passes` settles `queued` like `running` at the next daemon start, so nothing stays queued for good.
- **The detail draws the stages as one strip.** `MeetingDetailModel::stages` folds the row's status, the diarization block, the analysis status and the followed job into three stages (`transcript`, `speakers`, `analysis`), each `pending`, `queued`, `running`, `done`, `failed`, `unavailable` (the pass was wanted and the model set is not on disk) or `skipped` (switched off on purpose); `processingState` is `running` while any stage is unsettled, `failed` once all settled and one failed, `incomplete` when none failed and a pass was unavailable, `done` otherwise; `processingLine` reads `Transcript ready · speakers running`, `Processing complete`, `Processing complete · analysis not run`, `Processing incomplete · speakers skipped, no speaker model downloaded`, `Processing finished · analysis failed`. A ready speaker stage counts the segments the row carries without a `speaker_id` (`3 speakers · 154 segments unassigned`), because the pass labels a segment only under the coverage and share rule of ADR 0035 and the rest stays by source. `ProcessingStrip` sits under the header for the life of the meeting: the accent tone while running with a progress bar under the running stage, the urgent tone after a failure, the highlight tone when incomplete, and muted once every stage settled well, so the outcome and the stage facts stay on screen instead of a strip that vanishes and reads like success. The automatic speaker pass reports under a job id the screen never asked for and `job.progress` names no meeting, so while this meeting's pass is `queued` or `running` and no job is followed, the model reads `meetings.status`, which answers this meeting's running `job_diarize_<n>`, and follows that job alone; until it answers the bar stays a sweep and another meeting's chunks are never shown. Every read of the row carries a generation that `clear` and each new read bump, so two reads of one meeting that answer out of order never put a stale running pass back over the terminal state the newer read reported. The rail's analysis state gains `Analysis queued.` with no `Analyse now` while it waits, and the facts line adds `analysed`.
- **`meetings.rename` is the Linux verb for the title.** `{ meeting_id, title }` answers `{ ref, title, title_source: "manual" }`; control characters, an empty title and one over 200 characters are `INVALID_PARAMS` in that order, the row's FTS index follows through the 0004 trigger, and the method is allowed in every status. `DetailHeader` makes the title a focusable button named `Rename meeting` (its description is the title) that turns into a `TextField` named `Meeting title`; Return commits through `MeetingsActions::retitle`, Escape restores, an empty edit is refused before the daemon is asked, a refusal stays under the field with the edit kept, and `retitled` relabels the header, the list row (`MeetingsModel::retitle`, no reload) and nothing else. `t` is the key on the detail (free before this record; the footer hint names it) and typing in the field keeps every other detail key off, as the notes editor does.

## Consequences

- The tail can now show a window's fragments in the order the merger emitted them, remote first then microphone; a window that re-emits a shorter tail shortens it on the next `-p1`. A fragment that starts after the last final and is not re-announced stays until the next window of its source; the finalisation clears the screen either way.
- `finishing` may be true for a meeting the daemon still considers `recording` for the round trip of `meetings.stop`; the model's `recording` is false during that window so the meters stop reading levels.
- `queued` is a new status value on `meetings.get`, `meetings.list`, `transcripts.get`, `meetings.analysis.get` and the `meeting.state` transitions; clients that switch on the old spellings treat it as `none` through `parse`. The schema is at version 8; a store at 7 migrates on the next daemon start.
- The analysis plan is decided in `completed` and consumed there, so a `meetings.analyze` issued between `completed` and the automatic start races the plan the way it did before; the second start is refused `analysisRunning` and logged.
- The command line has no `dettivo meetings rename` verb yet; the method is on the socket and in `system.capabilities.meetings.methods`, and an agent reaches it through the daemon directly.
- The QML tests split: `tst_meeting_detail_processing.qml` and `app_meeting_detail_test.cpp` hold the detail's tests so each file stays under the length limit.

## Follow-up: returning to the meeting (2026-09-21)

Home and the meetings list offer **Return to meeting** while the live model owns a recording or its final transcription. The action opens that meeting without starting another capture. The list keeps its archived meetings available.

The live header reads the speech provider and model recorded by `meetings.get` after starting or attaching. The new-meeting rail restores the selected model when it opens, including when its provider list was loaded earlier. Picker defaults cannot label an existing recording.

A `completed` or `partial` event for the current meeting opens its detail even when the event also announces queued speaker or analysis work. The detail follows those passes. Once the live model releases the meeting, later pass updates cannot trigger a second completion navigation.

Automatic titles for newly started meetings use the host timezone at the meeting's start, including daylight-saving time; stored timestamps remain UTC and existing saved titles are unchanged. Saving a title clears its text selection and returns focus to the header without a title outline. Escape keeps keyboard focus on the rename control.
