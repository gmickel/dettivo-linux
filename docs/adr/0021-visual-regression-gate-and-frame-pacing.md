# 0021. Every surface is held to an approved baseline on every theme and scale by a blocking job with a canary; frame pacing is judged in the drives while a surface animates

Status: Accepted 2026-09-04; approved renders are held strictly and the canary has axes since [0052](0052-qa-isolation-and-honest-assertions-every-check-proves-what-it-claims.md)

## What this gives you

A control that drifts from the design, a stock widget that leaks in, a font that falls back or a layout that moves is caught by the merge gate on every theme and scale before a person sees it, re-approving a change is one recorded command, the gate proves on every run that it still sees differences, and every drive that shows a surface reports whether it dropped frames and how fast the theme switched.

## Situation

The OSD spec (ADR 0015) built the perceptual comparison and a per-state check for one surface on two themes, run as a CTest case and advisory during the fast build; the design system sheet only proved that it rendered. The strategy names blocking visual regression as the first gate of the cleanup phase, and the masterplan's QA-14 asks for every surface and state at 1x and 2x, dark and light, in the default theme plus two others, QA-16 for frame timings in the recording, transcribing and theme-switch drives, QA-17 for a negative style check over the rendered tree, and NFR-13 makes an unapproved difference a release blocker from the first baseline on. The comparison metric is blind to absolute colour by design, so that one dark artboard checks a light render; a colour-only regression therefore cannot fail it. The implemented sheet is laid out for the tokens rather than for the artboard's annotations and scores 0.44 against `design-system.png`, under the threshold, while the six pill states score 0.60 to 0.82 against their crops. The HANCORE Black Gold theme names a muted stone (`#6E6A58`) as `accent` in `colors.toml` while the artboards, the built-in palette and the shell's focus, pressed and bar-active tokens all use `#F5BF03`; with the stone accent the Listening pill scores 0.48. This desktop runs at 240 Hz under XWayland for the drives, where NFR-12 names 60, 120 and 144 Hz and asks for zero dropped frames and a render cost under a millisecond; a static window paints a handful of frames and the pill paints nothing while it is hidden or holding a state. CI renders under Xvfb, which has no real refresh.

## Decision

- `dettivo-qa visual` is the gate. `qa/visual/manifest.toml` lists every surface with its binary, its states and their `DETTIVO_E2E_*` variables, its artboard and crop rule, its threshold (per surface, never per run) and its theme set. Every Qt host takes `--render <png>` and writes its window on the offscreen platform after the first frame; the job renders every state at 1x and 2x (`QT_SCALE_FACTOR`) on the fixtures `black-gold`, `catppuccin-latte` and `tokyo-night` under `qt/fixtures/themes/` and on the built-in palettes `builtin-dark` and `builtin-light` (`DETTIVO_COLOR_SCHEME` pins the portal scheme), diffs each with `dettivo-visual-diff` at the surface's threshold, writes `visual-report.json` and `visual-report.md` with the diff images, and exits 1 on any entry that is not a pass. Xvfb is not needed for renders; the drives keep it.
- The baseline for an entry is the approved render `baselines/<surface>/<state>.<theme>.<scale>x.png`, else the Black Gold artboard crop for the artboard's theme (`baselines/<surface>/<state>.png`, cut by `scripts/design/crop-baseline.py` from the manifest's rule, or the artboard itself when the whole sheet is the crop), else nothing: the entry is `first-approval` with the exact approve command and the run fails. `dettivo-qa visual approve <surface> [--state --theme --scale]` renders fresh, refuses a render the style check named, copies it into place and appends who, when and the artboard score to `docs/design/baselines.md`; a re-approval is the same command, reviewed like any other change. The artboard score is reported on every theme as evidence for the beauty pass, not a gate.
- The design system sheet's baselines, Black Gold included, are approved renders with the artboard score 0.44 recorded, because closing the gap to the artboard is the beauty pass (S-36) and the gate's purpose here is that nothing changes unapproved; the pill's Black Gold baselines are the artboard crops. The `black-gold` fixture is the HANCORE theme's `colors.toml` and `shell.toml` with `accent` set to `#F5BF03`, the gold the artboards and the shell's own active states name, and says so in its header; whether Dettivo on the installed theme should read the stone accent is a question for the design-system spec, recorded there as a follow-up.
- The canary: `dettivo-qa visual --canary` renders the design system sheet with `DETTIVO_QA_CANARY=1`, which the theme honours only under QA mode: the accent moves to the cursor colour, the base and heading sizes rise by 8 and the spacing units by 3 and 5. The run passes only when every diff fails (scores 0.00 to 0.01 here). The accent nudge alone scores 0.98, which is the metric's colour blindness working as designed, so the canary carries the type and spacing change that the metric sees. The `visual` job of the rig workflow runs the real pass and then the canary and blocks the nightly and release runs (ADR 0040 moved it off the pull-request loop, where the workers' local run stands); `just qa-visual` and `just qa-visual-canary` are the same commands here.
- The negative style check runs in every `--render`: the host walks the window's visual tree; a control whose background or content item was created outside the `DettivoStyle` module, or a text whose font family is not `Theme.fontFamily`, fails the render with exit 3 and a `style-check:` line naming the item, and the report shows the entry as `style`. `DETTIVO_QA_PLANT=basic_control|default_font` plants one such item under QA mode so the check is proven on the sheet and the pill by CTest.
- Frame pacing: the collector is `qt/host/pacing`, linked into every Qt host and switched on by `DETTIVO_QA_PACING=<file>`, writing its summary once a second and at exit (swap timestamps, render cost, every theme change with the delay to the next swapped frame, `surface`); the drives add `scenario` and a `verdict`. The `osd_dictation` drive judges the pill from Listening to the stop of each take; the new `theme_switch` drive shows the pill and the app, replaces the theme directory as `omarchy theme set` does (stage, remove, move) and takes the palette's application plus its first frame as the switch time. A dropped frame is a swap interval of two periods or more at the slower of the display's rate and 144 Hz, the fastest rate NFR-12 names, counted only inside an animating stretch; the budget is two per thousand animated frames with a floor of two; the render cost at the 99th percentile stays under a millisecond once a hundred frames rendered; the switch applies within 100 ms. The thresholds gate on a real display and are recorded under CI (`CI` set), where the presence of the report is the gate.

## Consequences

- On this desktop (Hyprland, 240 Hz, the drives under XWayland): the theme switch applies in 2.8 to 7.3 ms for the pill and 1.8 to 2.4 ms for the app over five runs; the pill drops zero to one frame at 144 Hz in the 2.7 s window and one in 2 070 animated frames over the two takes of `osd_dictation`, render cost mean 0.02 ms, p99 0.04 to 0.11 ms. At the display's own 240 Hz the second take shows single-period misses at a 4 Hz cadence that the 144 Hz rule does not count; the cadence is recorded in every `pacing-dettivo-osd.json` and is the next thing to read when the pill's event handling is looked at.
- The visual matrix is 80 entries (8 states, 5 themes, 2 scales) and runs in 27 s here; the approved renders add 3 MB to the repository, most of it the sheet at 2x, and a surface's baselines grow by ten files per state.
- The gate was proven once on a throwaway branch: the theme's base size raised from 12 to 16 made `just qa-visual` exit 1 with six entries under the threshold (the pill's Listening, Transcribing and Copied states against their artboard crops at 0.46 to 0.47), and the same change passed the 74 entries held to approved renders, which bounds what the metric sees: a layout that moves, an element that goes missing or a font that reflows a row, not a type size that keeps the rows in place. Tightening that is a threshold or metric change in `dettivo-visual-diff`, recorded here when it happens.
- The pill's CTest visual case and `scripts/qa/osd-visual-diff.sh`, `scripts/design/crop-osd-baseline.py` and the `qa-osd-visual` recipe are gone; `just test-qt` proves renders and the style check, `just qa-visual` proves the pictures. The unit-qml CI job no longer covers the pill's pictures; the visual job does.
- The `DETTIVO_QA*` names grow by three (`DETTIVO_QA_PACING`, `DETTIVO_QA_PLANT`, `DETTIVO_QA_CANARY`), listed in both parsers and `docs/qa.md`; `DETTIVO_COLOR_SCHEME` joins `DETTIVO_REDUCED_MOTION` as a plain preference outside QA mode.
- The app's placeholder window draws its text on the application font; the style check names it the moment the Home route joins the manifest, which is the check doing its job.

The comparison/proof rules above are amended by [ADR 0054](0054-delete-duplicate-work-and-keep-one-owner.md). Approved renders now use full-resolution pixel colour comparison; allocation and aggregate GPU activity no longer establish inference attribution. Historical measurements and their original outcomes remain evidence of their stated method.
