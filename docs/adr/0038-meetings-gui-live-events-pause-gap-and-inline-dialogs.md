# 0038. The meetings screens are clients of the meeting events: the live model folds the segment stream, Pause waits for a verb, and the small dialogs are designed inline

Status: Accepted 2026-09-05, amended 2026-09-06 by 0048 (the notes draft stays dirty until acknowledged), amended 2026-09-06 by 0049 (the rail sends analyze and diarize only once changed, the picker sends provider and meeting_model)

## What this gives you

Meetings get their three screens on every Omarchy theme: the list by week with who spoke and what state each meeting is in, the live meeting with both meters, the elapsed time and a transcript that arrives while you talk, and the detail with the talk-time bar, the transcript with named speakers, your notes, the analysis, a rename that relabels the whole transcript, the five exports and a delete that says what it removes. Every screen is a view over the daemon's `meetings.*` methods and its three event topics, so what the window shows is what the command line and the agents see, and the drives prove the whole loop on both drivers.

## Situation

The meeting lane was complete on the daemon (ADR 0027, 0031, 0035, 0036) and the app's Meetings routes were scaffolds. Four artboards were approved for them (`meetings-list.png`, `meeting-live.png`, `meeting-detail.png`, `import-and-disclosure.png`), the History detail (ADR 0025) had set the pattern for a list beside a detail over the daemon, and the settings routes (ADR 0033) had set the pattern for a dialog designed inline. Three questions were open. How a live screen keeps a transcript in step with `meeting.segment`, whose provisional segments replace each other and whose finals interleave across two sources. What to do with the artboard's `Pause`, which the contract has no verb for. And how the visual gate holds a dialog the artboard draws beside another one, when the app centres each dialog over the page.

## Decision

- **The live model folds the event stream and follows the state topic into the detail.** `MeetingLiveModel` reads `meetings.disclosure.get` before a start and raises the dialog when the acknowledgement is missing; `meetings.start` carries the rail's `capture`, `expected_speakers`, `analyze` and `diarize` and the acknowledgement when the dialog gave it. `LiveSegmentsModel` keeps the finals of both sources ordered by start and at most one provisional segment per source, which the next window of that source replaces whole (`docs/meetings.md`); a final segment drops the tail it hardened out of. `audio.level` is split by its `source` into two meters; `job.progress` on the meeting's job carries the chunk count through `Stopping` and `Transcribing`; the first `completed` on `meeting.state` hands the meeting id to the route, which opens the detail. A `recording` event for a meeting the model does not know adopts it, so a meeting started from the bar or the command line shows on the live screen. The subscription is the app's one subscription (`StatusModel::topics`) with `meeting.state` and `meeting.segment` added.
- **Pause is on screen and disabled with its reason.** The contract's meeting has `start`, `stop` and `cancel`; the button keeps the artboard's place and its accessible description names the gap, so a drive and a screen reader read why. A pause verb, when the contract gains one, lands here without a layout change.
- **The small dialogs are designed inline against the pattern.** The rename popover (the name field, the remembered names from `meetings.speakers.suggest`, Enter applies, Escape cancels, an empty name restores the label), the meeting export sheet (the five formats and the engine's words), the stop confirmation and the delete confirmation (naming what `[meetings] delete_artifact_policy` removes) follow the History dialogs; the import and disclosure dialogs follow their artboard. Every dialog draws an opaque frame (`DialogFrame`), because the list behind the dimmed overlay showed through the alpha-layered surface and made the text hard to read.
- **The visual manifest gains `render_crop`.** A state may name the artboard's rectangle in `crop` and the render's rectangle in `render_crop`; the artboard crop is cut from the first, the render from the second, so the import and disclosure dialogs the artboard draws side by side are held against the dialogs the app centres over the page. The list column and the rail, the live header, transcript and rail, and the detail header, transcript and rail are regions of their artboards; the empty list, the Notes and Analysis tabs and the rename popover are approved renders. `DETTIVO_E2E_MEETING_STATE` picks the screen, the tab, the popover or the dialog a render shows, and `--render` with `DETTIVO_E2E_SEED=1` fills the models with the artboards' facts (`sample_meetings.cpp`).
- **The rail writes the daemon's selection and nothing else.** The speaker count and the analysis choice go on the start request alone; the engine menu writes the daemon's selection the way Settings does (`speech.selection.set` for another provider, `[speech] meeting_model` through `config.set` for a model of the selected one), because `meetings.start` takes no engine and the daemon runs one provider. The footer names `[meetings]` in `config.toml` as what sets the defaults and opens the Meetings settings section, which is the editor of those keys (ADR 0033). No new key joins the schema.
- **The history timeline gets the meeting facts it shows.** `transcripts.list` meeting rows carry `speaker_count`, `analysis_status` and `is_partial` as Linux additions (registered in `docs/api/linux-deltas.md`, omitted on a dictation row), so the History row's meta line reads `41 min · 3 speakers · analysed` from the page it already reads.
- **The import dialog takes a path, not a portal chooser.** The file is named in a field (the desktop's file chooser can join later without a layout change), probed on the host for its length when it is a WAV, uploaded through `transfer.begin`, `transfer.chunk` and `transfer.commit`, and started with `transcripts.import { target_kind: "meeting" }` carrying the engine, the language, `diarize`, `analyze` and the acknowledgement; the job is followed on `job.progress` and the detail opens when it ends. The estimate on screen is the file's length over an assumed eight times real time, stated as `About N minutes on this engine`.

ADR [0055](0055-completion-capture-and-client-actions-follow-authoritative-state.md) replaces the meetings-only `DialogFrame` with the shared style's opaque floating surface and removes the unused component. It also preserves live notes overrides and reconciles active meetings after connection or overflow.

## Consequences

- The live screen shows the segments the daemon sent since the model attached; a daemon restart keeps them on screen and the reconnect reads `meetings.status` for the meeting, landing on the partial row when the daemon promoted it. Segments sent while the app was away are not replayed; the finalisation fills the row.
- The rename popover, the export sheet and the two confirmations have no artboard; they are held as approved renders and by the drive, and the beauty pass can re-approve them like any other surface.
- `render_crop` is one more field in the manifest and one line in the matrix; `scripts/design/crop-baseline.py` keeps reading `crop` for the artboard.
- The three drives run on both drivers under Xvfb in the rig (`meetings_seeded`, `meetings_live_gui` with the mock tracks, `meetings_import_gui` with the clip); on a desktop with PipeWire the live drive rides the same capture the null sinks feed. `meetings_live_gui` records whether a provisional segment showed before the first final one, which depends on the engine keeping up with the tick.
- The engine menu changes the daemon's selection for dictation too when it picks another provider, as the Settings Models section does; a model of the selected provider changes the meeting model alone.
