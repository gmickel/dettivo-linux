# 0018. Parakeet runs on parakeet.cpp through an in-repo binding; its timestamps keep it dictation-only for now

Status: Accepted 2026-09-04

## What this gives you

A second speech engine that transcribes English (v2) or 25 European languages (v3) with word error rate parity to NVIDIA's NeMo release, on the same Vulkan backend as Whisper, from one `dettivo speech download --provider parakeet` away, and an honest capability matrix: the meeting lane keeps Whisper until Parakeet's word timestamps are measured within the merger's tolerance.

## Situation

ADR 0004 chose parakeet.cpp for the ggml family and left one question to a spike: are Parakeet's word timestamps precise enough to drive the meeting merger, which interleaves two audio sources by time and needs word offsets within 200 ms? The macOS and Windows ports gate Parakeet out of meetings for lack of timestamps. parakeet.cpp 0.5.0 (MIT, `mudler/parakeet.cpp`, tag `v0.5.0` at `1bfbebfa`, vendoring ggml `e705c5fe`) exposes a flat C API (`parakeet_capi.h`, ABI 6) that returns per-word start, end and confidence as JSON, publishes `q8_0` GGUF conversions of TDT 0.6B v2 and v3 that measure WER 0 against NeMo, and picks its ggml device (Vulkan or CPU) at first inference through `PARAKEET_DEVICE`. The C API carries no error text for a failed load, no thread setting and no way to reset the process-global backend; the TDT models ignore the language prompt and detect the language themselves; the library emits word times on its 80 ms encoder frame grid.

There was no golden word alignment for the test fixture. A reference forced aligner (torchaudio `forced_align` over wav2vec2 character CTC emissions, 20 ms frames) was run once over `jfk.wav` and the 22 word boundaries were checked in as `crates/dettivo-qa/fixtures/alignment/jfk.words.json`.

## Decision

- `crates/parakeet-cpp-sys` fetches the parakeet.cpp 0.5.0 and ggml archives at build time, verifies their SHA-256 (recorded in `build.rs`), applies upstream's CPU patch, builds them statically through CMake (`GGML_NATIVE=OFF`, OpenMP off; `PARAKEET_GGML_VULKAN=ON` under the `vulkan` feature), and binds `parakeet_capi.h` plus a 20-line C++ shim that exposes the backend's device name, thread count and shutdown. `PARAKEET_CPP_SOURCE_DIR` and `PARAKEET_CPP_ARCHIVE_DIR` point the build at a local tree or archives.
- `dettivo-engine-parakeet` speaks the engine protocol through the host loop now shared with Whisper in `dettivo-engine-proto` (`host`, `backend`), loads the model with half a second of silence so the backend and the weights are on the requested device before `load` answers, reports the device parakeet.cpp actually chose (a Vulkan device that fails to initialise is reported as the CPU fallback, and a failed Vulkan load resets the backend and reloads on the CPU unless `backend = "vulkan"` demands the GPU), reads `general.name` from the GGUF to know the model's languages (v2 English; v3 the 25 languages) and answers `bad_request` naming them for any other, chunks audio over 90 s into 60 s windows with 2 s overlap stitched at the overlap midpoint, groups words into segments at pauses over 700 ms and at 30 s, and carries `words` with confidence on every segment. `Segment.words` is an addition to the engine protocol, absent for engines that do not align words.
- The catalogue offers `parakeet-v2` and `parakeet-v3` (`q8_0`, checksums from the Hugging Face LFS pointers and verified against the downloaded files); `[engines.parakeet] backend` and `[engines.whisper] backend` choose `auto`, `vulkan` or `cpu` per engine; `speech.selection.set { provider: "parakeet" }` switches to `parakeet_model_id`.
- The spike's rule, fixed before the run in `dettivo_qa::alignment`: Parakeet is meeting-capable when every model measured pairs at least 90 % of the golden words and keeps the pooled start/end offset p95 within 200 ms and the median within 100 ms. The run (`docs/reports/parakeet-alignment-report.json`, Vulkan on an NVIDIA device, the same numbers on the CPU) paired 22 of 22 words for both models and measured v2 at a median of 84 ms and a p95 of 308 ms (starts: 42 ms median, 213 ms p95; ends: 98 ms median, 327 ms p95) and v3 at 123 ms and 347 ms. Word ends before a pause absorb the pause (`Americans,` ends 516 ms late), and starts sit on the 80 ms grid with a few 200 ms misses. **Parakeet is dictation-only**: `system.capabilities.speech.providers.parakeet.meeting_capable = false`, `speech.providers.list` reports `supports_meetings = false`, and the meeting specs keep Whisper.
- The route to change the decision is recorded so the next spike starts where this one stopped: correct the systematic emission lag with a per-model offset and clip word ends at the next word's start (the starts alone were within 213 ms p95 for v2), measure on more than one fixture, and re-run `dettivo-qa pipeline parakeet-alignment`; failing that, the Sherpa-ONNX route (the diarization engine spec brings Sherpa-ONNX in; NeMo's ONNX export of Parakeet runs there with the same word timestamps on the CPU) stays the documented fallback for Parakeet as ADR 0004 said.

## Consequences

- `cargo build --workspace` fetches two archives (about 5 MB) the first time and compiles ggml a second time; CI caches `target`, and the `parakeet-vulkan` job builds the Vulkan variant beside `whisper-vulkan`, advisory until the container proves a device.
- `scripts/models/fetch-test-model.sh` now fetches the 904 MB `parakeet-v2` model too, so the CPU word error rate fixture (`crates/dettivo-engine-parakeet/fixtures/wer.json`) runs in CI; the Vulkan rows run on a development machine (`just test-parakeet-vulkan`). Measured word error rate on `jfk.wav`: 0 for v2 on CPU and Vulkan, 0 for v3 on Vulkan.
- The Parakeet engine takes no vocabulary prompt (`supports_custom_vocabulary = false`); the daemon drops the prompt before the engine sees it.
- The golden alignment is one 11 s fixture aligned by a CTC model whose own boundaries carry error in the tens of milliseconds; the decision is honest for the tolerance asked and coarse for anything finer, which is why the report keeps the raw numbers beside the verdict.
- ADR 0004's expectation that Parakeet becomes meeting-capable on Linux is not met yet; the record there stays accurate about the mechanism and this record carries the outcome.
