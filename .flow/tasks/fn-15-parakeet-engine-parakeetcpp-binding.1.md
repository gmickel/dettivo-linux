## Done summary
Parakeet is the second speech engine: `crates/parakeet-cpp-sys` builds parakeet.cpp 0.5.0 (tag v0.5.0, commit 1bfbebfa) with its vendored ggml e705c5fe from archives fetched at build time and verified against SHA-256s pinned in build.rs (f0066d20... and 77eacb7c...), applies upstream's CPU patch, links statically (CPU always, ggml Vulkan under the `vulkan` feature) and binds `parakeet_capi.h` (ABI 6) plus a 20-line C++ shim for the backend device name, threads and shutdown; `dettivo-engine-parakeet` speaks the engine protocol and the CLI mode over a safe wrapper, picks Vulkan or CPU with the reason (a warm-up inference at load pins the device parakeet.cpp reports, a failed Vulkan load resets the backend and reloads on the CPU), reads `general.name` from the GGUF for the model's languages (v2 English; v3 the 25 European languages) and answers `bad_request` naming them, chunks audio over 90 s into 60 s windows with 2 s overlap stitched by word timestamps, groups words into segments at pauses, and carries `words` with confidence on every segment (`Segment.words`, a new optional field of the engine protocol with fixture `response.recognize.words.json`). The protocol loop, the backend choice and the WAV reader moved into `dettivo-engine-proto::host`/`backend` and the Whisper engine runs on them too. The catalogue offers `parakeet-v2` and `parakeet-v3` (checksums verified against the downloaded files: 2027e2e1... and 4d69a4a6...), `dettivo-speech` gained `ParakeetEngine` beside `WhisperEngine` with per-provider capabilities and the `meeting_capable` outcome, the daemon resolves model paths and engines per provider, `speech.providers.list` reports the real flags, `speech.selection.set { provider: "parakeet" }` switches to `parakeet_model_id`, `system.capabilities.speech.providers[].meeting_capable` is registered in the delta register, `[engines.whisper]`/`[engines.parakeet] backend` are new config keys, and the alignment spike (`dettivo-qa pipeline parakeet-alignment`) measured both engines against a golden wav2vec2 CTC forced alignment of jfk.wav under a rule fixed before the run: Parakeet v2 median 84 ms / p95 308 ms and v3 123 ms / 347 ms against the 200 ms tolerance, so Parakeet is dictation-only (ADR 0018, `docs/reports/parakeet-alignment-report.json`); WER on jfk is 0.000 for v2 on CPU and Vulkan and v3 on Vulkan.
