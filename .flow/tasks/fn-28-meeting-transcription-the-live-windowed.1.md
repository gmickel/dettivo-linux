## Done summary
fn-28 ships the live windowed path, the two-source merger, the `meeting.segment` events, finalisation over every take and recovery transcription, on top of the fn-24 capture branch. A previous worker landed the code (the windower, the per-source merger, the interleave, the contract payload and fixtures, the live transcriber thread, the daemon finalising on stop and on recovery, the config keys); this pass finished the proof, the surfaces and the record.

R1 is met by the unit tests in `crates/dettivo-transcribe` (`live/windower.rs`, `live/merger.rs`, `interleave.rs`, `live/mod.rs`): windows cut per tick with the overlap and never exceed the target, a lagging engine skips the oldest audio and silence is gated, provisional tails are replaced and finalise after the boundary gap, a gap rides on the first segment after it, the interleave orders by time with the remote side first on a tie and drops a microphone filler inside the padding of a remote span, and a window result with text but no segments fails naming the engine. `filters.rs` now also drops whisper.cpp's `[BLANK_AUDIO]` and its siblings, which the live drive showed as a provisional hallucination on a mostly silent window.

R2 is met by the new `meeting_live` drive (`crates/dettivo-qa/src/scenarios/meeting_live.rs`): the jfk clip plays as the other side through `DETTIVO_MOCK_SYSTEM_AUDIO` and its last phrase, after four seconds of silence, as the microphone through `DETTIVO_MOCK_MIC`, so the two overlap in one span; the scenario subscribes to `meeting.segment`, `meeting.state` and `job.progress`, asserts provisional then final per source with finals in time order and every payload on the registered shape, that `meetings.stop` answers `transcribing`, that `job.progress` climbs per chunk, and that the finalised transcript matches the two-source golden at `crates/dettivo-qa/fixtures/meeting-live/golden.txt` by word error rate per source (under 25 %; measured 0.0 for both) with the row's segments interleaved by start and `meetings.search` finding the text. It passed on this machine and CI runs it (plus `meeting_recovery`) in the drive job with the cached tiny.en. The lagging-engine case is covered by `crates/dettivo-meeting/tests/transcription.rs` with a slow fake engine (skips journaled, finalisation still complete). The engine's own segment boundaries decide which side's segment starts first when leading silence sits inside a take, so the drive holds the first side as evidence rather than a claim.

R3: `meeting_rig` and `meeting_recovery` now follow the stop and the recover into the finalisation and require the meeting to complete with at least one chunk per take (the rig with both microphone takes and the system track after the unload); `meetings.recover` answers `transcribing` and the row settles at `completed` with its audio and without the partial flag. The device switch with the gap marker on the second take's first segment is proven in `transcription.rs` and in the daemon tests. The daemon meeting tests are split into `meetings.rs`, `meetings_recovery.rs` and `common/meetings.rs` so every file is under the length limit (the previous worker's file had grown to 662 lines and the gate was red on it).

R4: `meetings.get`, `transcripts.get { kind: meeting }`, `meetings.search` and `transcripts.search` carry and find the finalised transcript (the daemon tests search for `country` after a live meeting), the exports name the side and the gaps in `txt` and `md` and carry every field in `json`.

R5: the `meeting.segment` snapshot fixture passes the events tests, `meeting.state` carries `live_segment_count`, `live_last_end_ms` and `is_finalizing`, and the pill now subscribes to `meeting.state`: a recording meeting shows Listening with the meeting clock as the hint (counting on from the `duration_ms` the event carried), the stop and the settled states hide it, a failed meeting shows `Meeting failed` with the reason. A host test and a Quick Test cover it.

R6: `docs/meetings.md` gains the Transcription section (the live tuning, the finalisation, the cross-source rule, the `segments` verb, the journal lines, the event payloads, the drive) and its state diagram, stop, recovery, history and event tables follow the new states; ADR 0030 records the live path sharing the offline merger, the finalisation from every take and the suppression rule, and is indexed; the config keys were already in the schema, the default file, `docs/config.md` and the `print_default` fixture; `dettivo meetings segments <id> [--follow]` is new in the CLI (one line per segment with side and span, `~` for a provisional live line, tested against the seeded daemon) and documented in `docs/meetings.md` and `docs/daemon.md`; the register, `docs/mcp.md`, `docs/dictation.md`, `docs/osd.md`, `docs/qa.md` and the README no longer point at a transcription spec still to come.
