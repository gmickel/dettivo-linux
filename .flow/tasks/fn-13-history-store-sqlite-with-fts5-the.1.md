## Done summary
Built the history store and everything that reads it. dettivo-storage is now a real crate: one SQLite database (`<data_dir>/dettivo.db`, `[history] db_path` moves it) opened in WAL mode with foreign keys on and a five second busy timeout through rusqlite with the bundled SQLite; numbered migrations compiled in from `migrations/`, recorded in `schema_migrations`, with a `VACUUM INTO` backup (`dettivo.db.bak-<version>`) written before the first pending migration touches an existing database and a failure that names the migration and the backup; a `dictations` table carrying every macOS DictationItem field (id, timestamps, app id and name, source kind dictation|audioImport|rerun, status transcribing|completed|failed, mode, provider, model, language, raw and final text, title, summary, duration, rerun_of_item_id, error code and message, audio path, insertion JSON); an FTS5 external-content index over raw text, final text, title and summary (unicode61, remove_diacritics 2) kept in step by triggers, queried as quoted prefix terms ANDed so word starts match like the macOS substring index, operator characters escaped, snippets from FTS5, cursor paging, score null, a 1024 byte query limit; keyset-paged listing with app and date filters; retention (retained takes move from the session directory into `<data_dir>/dictations/<id>/microphone.wav` with several takes concatenated, `metadata.json` beside it, an artifact policy keep|audio_only|none deciding what is written and what a delete removes, a sweep dropping audio past `audio_retention_days` and rows past `max_items` oldest first while skipping items a job uses); the twelve-item deterministic seed across three days and two apps whose newest row is the contract's sample dictation; JSON, Markdown and text renderers with checked-in goldens; and a stored ZIP writer and reader of its own so the archive export bundles items with their audio and restores into another profile with the same ids.

The daemon opens the store before the socket (a database it cannot read or migrate stops it with status 1 and the reason on stderr), hands every finished dictation to the store through a new `Archive` hook in dettivo-session before the final `dictation.state` is published (raw text now travels on the Transcript; a store that fails puts `history: <why>` on the idle state's reason and the insertion stands), seeds under `DETTIVO_E2E_SEED=1` idempotently, runs the sweep at start and hourly, and expires idle transfers each minute. `transcripts.list`, `get`, `latest`, `search` answer the contract over the dictation kind (meeting kinds are `NOT_IMPLEMENTED` until the meetings spec), `transcripts.import` and `export` move through `transfer.*` (chunked uploads spooled under `$XDG_CACHE_HOME/dettivo/transfers/` in order from seq 1 with `RATE_LIMITED_LOCAL` beyond `max_inflight`, SHA-256 checked on commit; exports rendered under `exports/` and pulled with `eof`), and three Linux additions join the catalog with fixtures and deltas rows: `transcripts.delete` (per the artifact policy, `CONFLICT` while a job uses the item), `transcripts.rerun` (retained audio transcribed again through the real engine with provider, model, mode and language overrides into a `rerun` item linked to the original; `job.progress` at 0 and 1; `NOT_FOUND` with `reason = audio_not_retained` without audio) and `transcripts.stats` for the doctor. `transcripts.export` gains `scope` item|range|all with `from`/`to` and the `zip` format (signalled by `history.archive_export`), `transcripts.list` gains `app_id`/`since`/`until`, and `system.capabilities.history` reports `fts = true`, `semantic_search = false`. Imports of non-WAV audio go through ffmpeg; a second import while one runs is the fixture's `CONFLICT importAlreadyInProgress`. `insert.perform` lands with text or `source_ref` from the store (guards answer `CONFLICT` until the insertion spec detects focus), `dictation.reinsert_last` falls back to the newest stored item after a restart, and `DETTIVO_MOCK_INSERT=1` now has an inserter that appends to `$XDG_STATE_HOME/dettivo/qa/inserted.txt`. `[history] keep_audio` (default true), `audio_retention_days`, `max_items`, `artifacts`, `db_path` replace `[audio] keep_dictation_audio` in the schema, the default file, the print_default fixture and docs/config.md; Paths gained `cache_dir`. `dettivo history list|get|latest|search|delete|rerun|export|import` drive the methods and the transfers, `dettivo doctor` prints the history row, the QA replay seeds its daemon and records the stateful fixtures with reasons, and docs/history.md plus ADR 0014 are the record.
