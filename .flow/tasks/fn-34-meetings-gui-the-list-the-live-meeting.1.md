# fn-34 Meetings GUI: the list, the live meeting, the detail, speakers and notes

Meetings get their three screens on every Omarchy theme, as clients of the daemon's `meetings.*` methods and its `meeting.state`, `meeting.segment` and `audio.level` streams: the list by week beside the new-meeting rail, the live meeting with both meters, the elapsed display and a transcript that arrives provisional then final, and the detail with the talk-time bar, the tabs with the Raw and Polished toggle, the analysis rail, the rename popover, the export sheet and the urgent delete. The import and disclosure dialogs follow their artboard; the QML lives under `qt/qml/Dettivo/app/meetings/` over `MeetingsModel`, `MeetingLiveModel` with `LiveSegmentsModel`, `MeetingDetailModel` and `MeetingsActions` in `qt/host/app/`; ADR 0038 records the decisions.

**R1** is met by `meetings_seeded` (`crates/dettivo-qa/src/scenarios/meetings_seeded.rs`): over the seeded daemon the drive reads the two week labels, the four rows with their chips (`Analysed`, `Partial`, `Notes only`, `Transcript`) and the swatches `Gordon, Mara`, opens the roadmap review and reads the talk-time bar (`Rename Gordon`, `Rename Mara`), the tabs, `Raw` and `Polished`, the analysis rail with its blocks and the exports row, writes the Markdown export through the sheet into `DETTIVO_E2E_EXPORT_DIR` and compares it byte for byte with `crates/dettivo-storage/tests/goldens/meeting.md`, renames Mara through the popover (the bar and the transcript relabel, the store carries the name), and deletes the notes-only meeting after the urgent confirmation so the list shows three rows without a reload. Every capture runs the negative text scan. Verified locally under Xvfb on the in-repo driver (11.6 s, pass); the rig runs it on both drivers.

**R2** is met by `MeetingLiveModel` and `meetings_live_gui`: on a fresh profile with `DETTIVO_E2E_DISCLOSURE=pending`, Start meeting reads `meetings.disclosure.get` and raises the disclosure dialog with the macOS message; Not now leaves the list without a meeting (the drive checks `system.health`); Acknowledge and start puts the acknowledgement on `meetings.start`, the live screen shows `Mic meter`, `System meter`, `Elapsed`, `Pause` (disabled, the reason in its description) and `Stop`; the mock tracks reach the transcript as rows named `You: …` and `Remote: …`, provisional rows carry the `provisional` mark beside their time and the drive records that a provisional row showed before the first final one; Stop asks once, the header reads `Stopping` then `Transcribing · n of m chunks` from `job.progress`, and the first `completed` on `meeting.state` lands on the detail with the transcript. A refused start names the daemon's gate (`sessionActive`, `engineWithoutTimestamps`, `meetingDisclosureRequired`) under the column labels. Verified locally under Xvfb (46.8 s, pass, `meetings-live-gui.json`); the host test covers the gate, the start, the levels per source, the notes debounce and the stop against the fake link.

**R3** is met by `MeetingsActions::importFile` and `meetings_import_gui`: the dialog maps the file, the target kind, the engine, the language, `diarize after transcription` and `summary, decisions, action items` onto `transcripts.import { target_kind: "meeting" }` after a `transfer.begin`, `transfer.chunk`, `transfer.commit` upload, shows the chunking sentence with the estimate from the file's length, follows `job.progress` and opens the detail when the job ends. The drive first imports a file that is not audio and reads the daemon's reason in `Import refused: …`, then the jfk clip, waits for the fifth row, the detail and the `You: …` transcript row, and checks the row's transcript carries the clip's words. Verified locally under Xvfb (pass; `meetings-import-gui.json`).

**R4** is met by the six surfaces in `qa/visual/manifest.toml`: `meetings` (the list column and the rail out of `meetings-list.png`), `meeting-live` (header, transcript, rail out of `meeting-live.png`), `meeting-detail` (header, transcript, rail out of `meeting-detail.png`), `meeting-dialogs` (the import and disclosure boxes out of `import-and-disclosure.png`, held against the dialogs' own rectangles through the new `render_crop` field), `meetings-empty` and `meeting-detail-tabs` (the Notes and Analysis tabs and the rename popover as approved renders). Every Black Gold region scores above the 0.55 threshold against its artboard crop (list 0.70, rail 0.85, live 0.56 to 0.85, detail 0.69 to 0.90, import 0.73, disclosure 0.58) at 1x and 2x; the other four themes and the artboard-less surfaces were approved through `dettivo-qa visual approve` and recorded in `docs/design/baselines.md`; `DETTIVO_E2E_MEETING_STATE` picks the screen a render shows and `--render` with `DETTIVO_E2E_SEED=1` fills the models from `sample_meetings.cpp`. The speaker colours come from `Theme.roleSpeakerColors`. Verified by `dettivo-qa visual --surface <name>` for all six surfaces on all five themes at both scales.
