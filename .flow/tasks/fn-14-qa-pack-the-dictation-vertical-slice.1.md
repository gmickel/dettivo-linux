## Done summary
### Summary
Built the dictation pack, the first release-shaped report. `dettivo-qa pack <name>` (with `pack list`) runs a named, ordered list of steps in one evidence run directory (`qa-evidence/<run>/pack-<name>/`) and writes `<name>-pack.json` plus `<name>-pack.md`: a Pack is steps with a scenario id or the Whisper WER check, a pinned driver or the run's `--driver`, an expectation (`must pass` or `skip allowed`) and the measurement it contributes; a hard failure (a failed step, or a skip on a must-pass step) stops the pack and marks every later step `not_run` naming the failed step unless `--continue`; exit 0 on pass or allowed skips, 1 on a hard failure, 2 on a preflight refusal (an unknown pack, which lists the packs; an unbuilt dettivod; an unwritable evidence directory). The dictation pack composes, in order, `hotkeys_hyprland`, `osd_dictation` on atspi and on cua, `insertion_matrix`, `never_into_self`, the new `history_roundtrip` (must pass), the new `first_insert_timing`, and `whisper_wer` (must pass). The preflight starts a daemon for `system.capabilities.platform` (real chain, so the insertion backend is the desktop's), runs the doctor checks and records every failing display, session-bus or accessibility-bus check as a blocker up front; every step that drives a window then skips with that reason instead of failing against a dead bus mid-scenario. `dettivo-qa doctor` now connects to the address `org.a11y.Bus` answers, so a launcher that died (this desktop's state today) is named rather than trusted.

`history_roundtrip` dictates jfk.wav through the mock microphone, finds the item with `transcripts.search`, restarts the profile's own daemon, reads the item back with `transcripts.get`, and `dictation.reinsert_last` inserts it again through the mock target; `inserted.txt` holds two equal lines matching the stored text (`roundtrip.json`). `first_insert_timing` runs eleven dictations of the fixture into the Qt insert target over the real chain with the target guard, measures stop-request-to-completion-event on its own clock per run, splits it with the new `timings` block, excludes the cold run, and writes p50/p95 (nearest rank), the split's p50 and the Whisper backend from `speech.engines` into `first-insert-timing.json`; fewer than ten completed warm runs fail it with the count. `dictation.state`'s completion transition gains the Linux addition `timings` (`capture_ms`, `transcribe_ms`, `insert_ms`), measured in the session worker (drain and finish of the take after the stop, the engine's recognize, the inserter's call), carried on `Transcript`/`StateChange`, typed as `DictationTimings` in dettivo-proto, absent on every other transition, registered in `docs/api/linux-deltas.md`. The report aggregates `insertion-matrix.json` into `insertion_reliability` (passes over attempted, skipped targets excluded and listed, failed targets with backend and reason) against NFR-3 0.98, the timing runs against NFR-1 (GPU tier p50 at or under 1000 ms; the CPU tier has no target yet and records only), and the WER against 0.15; the tier is `gpu` only when the Whisper engine reported Vulkan. The NFR comparison is recorded, never gated (CI or here); the report says so. `just qa-pack <name>` plus the Makefile mirror (`make qa-pack PACK=dictation`); the CI drive job runs `scripts/qa/xvfb-session.sh just qa-pack dictation` after the individual drives and the existing upload carries the report. `docs/qa.md` has the pack section and the two scenario rows, `docs/RELEASING.md` starts with the pack as gate step 1 and the rule that the blocker list names every external blocker, ADR 0017 records the pack model, the tier rule, the timing split and the never-gates decision; README lists RELEASING.md.
