## Done summary
A meeting is now a record: the user's notes and the model's analysis live apart on the row and beside the audio, one search finds a word in the title, the transcript, the notes or the analysis and says which, the five exports render with speakers and timestamps against goldens, and a delete removes exactly what the contract's policy names. ADR 0036 records the decisions; every `meetings.*` fixture replays against the seeded daemon.

**R1 (notes).** Migration `0005-notes-analysis` adds the notes and analysis columns. `meetings.notes.set` writes the row and `notes.md` in the meeting directory (through a sibling temporary file and a rename); `meetings.notes.get` reads them back with the source (`user`, `live`) and the time; notes are allowed on a recording or partial meeting; a body over 1 MiB is `INVALID_PARAMS` naming the limit. The rebuilt FTS5 index covers `notes_markdown` and `analysis_text`, so a word from the notes is found with `matched_field = notes`. Verified by `crates/dettivod/tests/meetings_notes.rs` (notes on the row, the file, the search column, the fixtures, the 1 MiB refusal) and the analysis test, which asserts the notes are unchanged after `meetings.analyze`.

**R2 (analysis).** `dettivo-language::analysis` carries the prompt contract, the strict parser (thinking blocks and fences stripped, macOS and camel-case keys accepted), the chunking on segment lines at `[meetings.analysis] chunk_chars` with one merge call (map, then reduce), and one repair pass before a run fails naming both reasons. The daemon's `Analysis` service runs it as a job over the same `LlmProvider` layer as Enhanced, stores the result on the row and in `analysis.json`, reports `analysis_status` on `meeting.state`, and starts on its own after every finalisation (and import) when `[meetings.analysis] auto` is on. `force` regenerates and a failed regenerate keeps the old analysis. Verified by the goldens under `crates/dettivo-language/tests/goldens/meeting_analysis.json` (seven cases: clean, fenced and camel-case answers, repair, double failure, empty transcript, timeout, map then reduce with the call and part counts), by `meetings_notes.rs` with `DETTIVO_MOCK_LLM=fixture:<dir>` (`chunk_chars = 200` runs `parts=2`, timeout is `failed` with `provider_unavailable` keeping the old analysis, a degenerate answer fails after the repair pass, an empty transcript never reaches the model, no provider answers the notice), and by `llm_local.rs`, which analyses the seeded roadmap meeting through the real `llm/qwen3-1.7b` engine and skips by name without it.

**R3 (export).** The renderers label every segment with its speaker or side, mark gaps, prefix `srt` and `vtt` cues, put a Notes section (the live editor's `notes_override`, then the saved notes) above the Analysis section in `md`, and render the FR-G8 keys (`id`, `title`, `started_at`, `ended_at`, `duration_ms`, `status`, `language`, `stt_provider_id`, `stt_model_id`, `transcript`, `segments`, `analysis`) plus `notes` and `speakers` in `json`; `raw = true` renders the engine's words. The finalisation fills `polished_text` per segment through the deterministic Polish pass so the transcript is the polished join. Verified by `crates/dettivo-storage/tests/meeting_export.rs` (byte-equal to `tests/goldens/meeting.{txt,md,srt,vtt,json}`, the precedence, `raw`), the CLI export test, and `meetings_import.rs` (an unknown format is `INVALID_PARAMS`; `zip` on a meeting lists the five).

**R4 (delete).** `transcript_only` clears the texts, segments, speakers, analysis, `analysis.json` and the search entry and keeps the row, the notes and every take; `transcript_and_audio` also removes the WAVs and the take sidecars and forgets the audio facts, keeping the row's facts and the notes; `all` removes the row and the directory; the active meeting is `CONFLICT` `sessionActive`; a running analysis is cancelled first. `[meetings] delete_artifact_policy` is the CLI default. Verified by `crates/dettivod/tests/meetings_delete.rs` per policy over a laid-out meeting directory.

**R5 (contract and CLI).** Fixtures for `meetings.notes.get`, `notes.set`, `analyze`, `analyze.error-conflict-not-completed` and `analysis.get`, and the updated `get`, `list` (the four seeded meetings), `search` (`matched_field`), `transcripts/get`, `transcripts/search`, `transcripts/stats` and `system/capabilities` (`meetings.methods` lists the four) pass in `dettivo-proto`'s round trip, the daemon's contract test and `dettivo-qa contract` (0 pending). `dettivo meetings notes get|set`, `analyze`, `analysis`, `export`, `delete --policy` and `disclosure --copy` are covered by `crates/dettivo-cli/tests/meetings.rs` with `--json` snapshots equal to the fixtures; `--copy` lands the macOS text in the mock sink. `DETTIVO_E2E_DISCLOSURE=acknowledged|pending` seeds or clears the acknowledgement (daemon, Qt list and `docs/qa.md`), verified in `meetings_notes.rs`.

**R6 (documentation).** `docs/meetings.md` gains Notes, Analysis, Search, Export, Delete and The disclosure sections; `docs/config.md` documents `[meetings.analysis]` and `delete_artifact_policy`, which `config print-default` prints (the fixture is regenerated); `docs/qa.md` documents the variable and the seed; `docs/api/linux-deltas.md` registers the methods and fields; ADR 0036 records the analysis path, the polished segments and the delete policy and is indexed.

The macOS analysis prompt text was not available in this checkout (no macOS repo locally, nothing in the vault); the prompt is this port's wording of the same output contract, and the ADR says so. The migration is numbered `0005` because the migration list must be gapless; if the diarization branch lands its own `0005` first, this one renumbers to `0006`. Speaker names, the speaker pass and the `diarization` export block belong to the diarization spec: every renderer here reads the `speaker` label the row carries and falls back to the side. The GUI surfaces (notes editor, analysis rail, export sheet, disclosure dialog) are S-27. A three-segment `config.set` key such as `meetings.analysis.auto` follows the existing `[engines.llm]` precedent and is edited in the file.
