## Done summary
Built the meeting capture core: a meeting is two takes on one clock, a journal and a live checkpoint, and a daemon that dies mid-meeting promotes it to a partial meeting at its next start. dettivo-meeting (new crate; depends on proto, audio, storage; edge table updated) holds the state machine (recording, stopping, stopped, cancelled, failed, partial): start takes the single meeting slot under a lock, opens the microphone (a pinned `[audio] input_device` or the default source; required) and the system track (`[audio] system_source`: the default sink's monitor through the new `Target::SystemMonitor`, followed when the default sink changes, or a pinned sink; a missing one journals `system_unavailable` and the meeting is room audio), stores the row as `recording`, and a worker drains both sources into `microphone.wav` and `system.wav` under `<data>/meetings/<id>/` through two TakeWriters sharing the meeting's Instant (`takes.json` and `system-takes.json` carry `start_offset_ms` and `start_offset_ns` on one base), meters both tracks onto `audio.level` with `source = microphone|system`, writes `journal.jsonl` (start, system_unavailable, checkpoint, device_switch, device_lost, gap, system_switch, system_ended, error, stop, cancel, recovered) and `live-checkpoint.json` (schema 1: id, start, both tracks' takes with offsets and flushed samples, the empty segment tail, next_sequence, chunks_completed/total, is_finalizing, last_error) atomically every `[meetings] checkpoint_interval_seconds` beside a flush of both takes (a failed write is logged and the next interval retries). A microphone that vanishes (the default moved, a pinned node left the PipeWire graph, a stream ended) closes the take, reopens on the current default (a pinned device that is gone falls back to the default) and starts `microphone-2.wav` with `gap_before` and a `gap` journal line; a loss that finds no source records the gap and the system track carries on. `stop` answers `stopping` at once and the worker reports `stopped` (a second stop answers the same, a stop on a settled meeting answers its state, never an error); cancel removes the directory; a take that cannot be written ends the meeting `failed` with its takes kept. `metadata.json` (the row, both take lists, the checkpoint schema) is written under `artifacts = keep`; `keep_audio = false` removes the takes at stop; `none` removes the directory.

Recovery: before the socket listens the daemon promotes every row still `recording`/`stopping` to `partial`: each WAV header is repaired from the file length (`repair_wav`; a SIGKILLed hound writer never finalises), the duration is the longer track, the checkpoint gives `chunks_completed`/`chunks_total`, `is_partial = true`, a `recovered` journal line, and a checkpoint that cannot be read leaves 0/0 with `recovery_reason` naming why. `meetings.status` carries a Linux `capture` block and a `recoverable` list; `meetings.recover` keeps the meeting as `stopped` with its audio, `meetings.discard` removes it; either on a non-partial meeting is `CONFLICT meetingNotPartial`. Gates on `meetings.start`, in order: `sessionActive` (a dictation or a meeting; a dictation during a meeting is refused with the contract fixture's message), `meetingDisclosureRequired` (the fixture's kind; the macOS text through `meetings.disclosure.get`, recorded once with its time in `state.toml` by `meetings.disclosure.acknowledge` or `acknowledge_meeting_disclosure = true`, on imports too), `engineWithoutTimestamps` (a provider ADR 0018 keeps dictation-only; details carry the provider and the `settings.models` route). `meetings.delete` is implemented with the contract's `artifact_policy` (`retention.delete = true`; the reserved-shape fixture retired). Store: migration `0004-meetings` (the macOS MeetingSession fields plus the recovery fields, FTS5), `transcripts.list` walks one timeline over both kinds with a `created_at|kind|seq` cursor, `transcripts.get|latest|search|delete|cancel` answer the meeting kind, `transcripts.export` renders a meeting in txt/md/json/srt/vtt, `transcripts.import { target_kind: meeting }` runs the chunked pipeline into a meeting row after the disclosure gate and answers `is_partial = false`; a meeting re-run stays `NOT_IMPLEMENTED` for the transcription spec. The seed carries the contract's sample meeting so the `meetings.*` and meeting-kind `transcripts.*` fixtures replay; `MEETING_PENDING` is gone and the stateful ones (start, stop, cancel, status, delete, recover, discard, disclosure.acknowledge, the engine gate, import, export) are covered by name. `meeting.state` carries the Linux states and bounded metadata; `HistoryStatus` gains `stopping`, `stopped`, `partial`; `audio.devices` reports `default_sink`; `system.capabilities.meetings` lists the additions with `checkpoint_schema = 1`. CLI: `dettivo meetings start|stop|cancel|status|list|get|search|delete|recover|discard|disclosure [--acknowledge]` and `dettivo audio devices`; MCP: the four meeting tools answer, the harness reads the seeded meeting through `list_meetings`, `get_meeting` and `meeting://{id}`.
